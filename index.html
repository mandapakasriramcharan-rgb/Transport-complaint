WHY ROUTEWISE

From scattered complaints to actionable decisions.

RouteWise connects students, transport teams and administrators through one structured complaint system.

01

Students report

Submit transport problems with route, location, priority, description and optional photo evidence.

02

RouteWise analyses

Complaints are classified, prioritised and checked for duplicate or recurring issues.

03

Teams respond

Administrators assign issues, monitor SLA deadlines and record resolution actions.

04

Campus improves

Route health, hotspots and complaint trends reveal where transport improvements are needed.
